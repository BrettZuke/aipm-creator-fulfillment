# Who buys

Copy to `AUDIENCE.md`. Read by the sales-call intelligence brief.

Write this FROM THE CALLS, not from the client's idea of their audience. The two are usually
different, and the gap between them is often the most valuable thing the brief finds.

Cover:

- **Who they actually are.** Age, work, country, situation. In plain words.
- **What they say they want**, in their own phrasing, taken verbatim from transcripts.
- **What they are afraid of.** Usually said once, late in a call, quietly.
- **What they have already tried**, and what it cost them. Someone burned before buys on
  proof; someone fresh buys on promise, and the same script cannot do both.
- **What they liked before they liked the offer.** Often the person, not the mechanism. If
  that is true here, say so plainly: it changes every piece of copy downstream.

⚠️ Quote them. A summary in your words loses the exact phrasing, and the exact phrasing is
the part worth putting into copy.
