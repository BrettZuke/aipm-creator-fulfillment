# Scripting Toolkit

Built from the Content Strategist Brain. Open this to script fast: proven hook templates, reusable
reel and carousel skeletons, and the rules for making a piece good before it ships.

The bracketed placeholders are the point. A template is a shape, not a sentence: fill it with the
one specific thing only this client can say, never copy an opener word for word.

⚠️ The worked examples below happen to be drawn from AI and automation, because that is the
lane they were collected in. **The subject matter is illustrative and the structure is the
product.** Read past the topic to the shape underneath: a hook that works by promising a
specific number in a specific timeframe works the same for a fitness coach, a roofer or a
photographer. Swapping the topic in is the job; copying the topic across is the mistake.

# Hook Swipe File

## The Specific Result / Number Promise
WHY: Instantly communicates tangible value and sets clear expectations, appealing to goal-oriented individuals.
- Get [SPECIFIC RESULT] in just [TIMEFRAME] with this [AI TOOL/AUTOMATION].
- Here are the [NUMBER] [AI AGENTS/CODING HACKS] I use to get [DESIRED OUTCOME].
- I generated [DOLLAR AMOUNT] in [TIMEFRAME] using this [COLD CALLING SCRIPT/WEBSITE STRATEGY].
- Unlock [BIG BENEFIT] with these [NUMBER] steps for [AI TOOL/AUTOMATION].
- The [NUMBER] most crucial metrics for [BUSINESS GOAL] you need to track.
- My [NUMBER]-step process to scale [BUSINESS TYPE] to [REVENUE GOAL] per month.

Steal these openers:
- I got 80+ million views in the last 6 months
- £5400 contacted revenue signed in just 7 days with online coaching.
- I’ve posted 500 times over 6 years and run a $500K/year personal brand. Here’s the truth about what ACTUALLY gets you clients from content.
- I make $200k/m coaching in only 8 calls/week

## The Contrarian Callout / Myth Bust
WHY: Challenges audience assumptions, creating immediate engagement and positioning the creator as an authority with a unique perspective.
- Everyone says [COMMON AI ADVICE], but here's why that's wrong for [TARGET NICHE].
- [OUTDATED BELIEF] is a scam. I made [IMPRESSIVE RESULT] using it differently.
- [AI/AUTOMATION TOOL] will not [COMMON BELIEF]. It [ACTUAL FUNCTION].
- The [COMMON PRACTICE] is the worst advice in [INDUSTRY] right now.
- You don’t need [OVERHYPED SOLUTION]. There’s a better way to [ACHIEVE GOAL].
- Most [TYPE OF BUSINESS] don’t need more [RESOURCE].

Steal these openers:
- AI will not build your personal brand for you. It amplifies the one you already have.
- “You are the niche” is the worst advice in content right now.
- Most coaches think they have an ad problem, when in reality, it’s something completely different and very hard to find.
- Society has been engineered to sell you fake artificial substitutes to all of our basic human needs.

## The Curiosity Gap Question
WHY: Piques innate human curiosity by posing a question or a contradiction, compelling viewers to watch for the answer.
- Have you ever wondered how some [AI EXPERTS/GROWTH OPERATORS] get [AMAZING RESULT] while others struggle with [COMMON PAIN]?
- What if I told you [COMMON ASSUMPTION] about [AI/AUTOMATION] was completely false?
- [RESULT A] vs. [RESULT B]: The difference isn't [OBVIOUS FACT]... it's [HINT].
- Is it possible to [ACHIEVE CHALLENGING GOAL] with just [LIMITED RESOURCE]?
- What are [KEY CONCEPT] and how can you use them to get [DESIRED OUTCOME]?
- Are you on track to [GOAL] or a little off course with your [STRATEGY]?

Steal these openers:
- Same content can do 2.2M views or 2,455. The difference isn’t the content…
- Wanna get rich or famous?
- Riddle me this
- What are Brand Outliers and how can you use them to get more views and stand out online?

## The How To / Instructional Value
WHY: Directly addresses audience needs for practical solutions, offering immediate utility and positioning the creator as a helpful guide.

- The single most effective [STRATEGY/DRILL] to improve your [SKILL/METRIC].
- How to optimize your [PROFILE/FUNNEL] for [FOLLOWERS/LEADS].
- [NUMBER] steps to [DESIRED RESULT] without [COMMON STRUGGLE].
- How NOT to [NEGATIVE ACTION] when [SITUATION].
- My [NUMBER]-minute guide to [MASTER A SKILL] like a pro.

# Script Skeletons

## Reel Skeletons

Here are 8 reusable reel structures, designed for quick scripting and high retention:

### 1. The 3-Shot Proof

A concise, high-impact structure to demonstrate a quick win with undeniable visual proof.

* **Beat-by-beat:**
 * **SHOT 1 (Talking Head, 0-3s): HOOK**
 * LINE: "STOP wasting hours on [Pain Point]!"
 * Visual: Big bold condensed caps text overlay: "STOP [PAIN POINT]"
 * **SHOT 2 (Screen Recording / Phone Mockup, 3-8s): VISUAL PROOF**
 * LINE: (No talking, upbeat music)
 * Visual: Quick, undeniable demonstration of the stated outcome. Actual software, dashboard, or phone notification showing the result.
 * **SHOT 3 (Talking Head, 8-15s): QUICK PAYOFF + CTA**
 * LINE: "This [AI/automation] workflow automates [X]% of the busywork. Wanna see how?"
 * Visual: Big bold condensed caps text overlay: "COMMENT [KEYWORD]"
* **Ideal Length:** 10-18 seconds
* **Hook/CTA Placement:** Hook is Shot 1 (first 1-2s). CTA is Shot 3 (final 3-5s).
* **Micro-Example (AI Lead Qualification):**
 * **SHOT 1:** (Talking Head) "YOU'RE WASTING HOURS on lead qualification every week!"
 * *Visuals:* Persistent Title: "AUTOMATE LEAD QUALIFICATION". Progress bar. Word-by-word captions. Big bold text overlay: "STOP MANUAL QUALIFICATION" (Yellow pop on "WASTING HOURS").
 * **SHOT 2:** (Screen recording of an AI agent chatting with a prospect, pulling key info, then a CRM screen showing lead status updated to "Qualified AI Verified")
 * **SHOT 3:** (Talking Head) "This AI-powered workflow automates 90% of the busywork. Want the setup guide?"
 * *Visuals:* Big bold text overlay: "COMMENT AIQUAL" (Yellow pop on "90%").

### 2. Problem to Payoff

A detailed structure that hooks with a problem, introduces a solution, demonstrates it, and shows clear results.

* **Beat-by-beat:**
 * **SHOT 1 (Talking Head, 0-3s): HOOK**
 * LINE: "Most founders are DROWNING in [Problem Area]!"
 * Visual: Big bold condensed caps text overlay: "DROWNING IN [PROBLEM]"
 * **SHOT 2 (Talking Head, 3-7s): LEAD**
 * LINE: "It kills your productivity, burns out your team, and costs a fortune."
 * **SHOT 3 (Screen Recording / Data Viz, 7-15s): PROBLEM AMPLIFIER**
 * LINE: (No talking, showing the struggle)
 * Visual: Data visualization, problematic current tool setup, or screenshot of overwhelming manual process.
 * **SHOT 4 (Talking Head, 15-20s): SOLUTION INTRO**
 * LINE: "But there's a simple AI fix that handles [X]% automatically."
 * **SHOT 5 (Screen Recording / Floating Apps, 20-35s): SOLUTION IN ACTION**
 * LINE: (Narrating the build live)
 * Visual: Step-by-step screen recording of building the solution, showing real software usage (e.g., Zapier, Custom GPT, automation platform).
 * **SHOT 6 (Phone Mockup / Dashboard, 35-40s): THE PAYOFF**
 * LINE: (No talking, showing success)
 * Visual: Display the measurable result or final output (e.g., automated chat resolved, dashboard with green metrics).
 * **SHOT 7 (Talking Head, 40-45s): CTA**
 * LINE: "This AI automation gives you back hours every day. Want the full blueprint?"
 * Visual: Big bold condensed caps text overlay: "COMMENT [KEYWORD]"
* **Ideal Length:** 40-50 seconds
* **Hook/CTA Placement:** Hook is Shot 1 (first 1-2s). CTA is Shot 7 (final 5s).
* **Micro-Example (AI Customer Support):**
 * **SHOT 1:** (Talking Head) "Most founders are DROWNING in customer support requests!"
 * *Visuals:* Persistent Title: "AI SUPPORT AUTOMATION". Progress bar. Word-by-word captions. Big bold text overlay: "DROWNING IN SUPPORT" (Red pop on "DROWNING").
 * **SHOT 2:** (Talking Head) "It kills your productivity, burns out your team, and costs a fortune."
 * **SHOT 3:** (Screen recording of overflowing email inbox, manual copy-pasting into helpdesk, then a graph showing escalating support ticket volume.)
 * **SHOT 4:** (Talking Head) "But there's a simple AI fix that handles 80% automatically."
 * **SHOT 5:** (Screen recording showing connecting an AI agent to Slack, feeding it knowledge base articles, and setting up auto-response rules in real time.)
 * **SHOT 6:** (Phone mockup of a Slack channel with AI bot successfully resolving complex customer query, followed by a dashboard showing 80% reduction in human-handled tickets.)
 * **SHOT 7:** (Talking Head) "This AI automation gives you back hours every day. Want the full blueprint?"
 * *Visuals:* Big bold text overlay: "COMMENT AIBOT" (Yellow pop on "80%"). Slam text cards: "PROBLEM", "SOLUTION", "PAYOFF".

# The Director's Playbook

## The Non-Negotiables

* Stack verbal, written, and visual hooks in the first three seconds.
* Focus each piece on a single, hyper-specific idea relevant to your build process.
* Demonstrate concepts visually; show the outcome, do not just describe it.
* Lead with a contrarian take or a common mistake specific to your AI/automation niche.
* Keep spoken scripts under 75 seconds and read them aloud for natural flow.
* Include a single, clear verbal call to action.
* Solve one specific pain point for your target automation-builder audience.
* Target a niche audience; do not chase broad virality.
* Signal credibility with a brand outlier in the first five seconds.
* Ensure content delivers high perceived value to your audience.
* Apply consistent brand fonts, colors, and visual style for instant recognition.

## The Weekly Operating Rhythm

The "4-Hour Content Week" generates 5-7 distinct content pieces. The rhythm is built for efficiency, data-driven formats, and repurposing:

* **Monday (1 hour): Idea & Research.** Generate 5-7 validated content ideas by mining client conversations, reflecting on personal struggles, and observing market trends. Utilize the "Scroll Save Method" to curate high-performing competitor content and use tools like Sort Feed to identify outlier videos (5x creator's follower count) for templating.
* **Tuesday (1 hour): Scripting.** Draft full scripts or detailed outlines for 5-7 videos. Include 5-10 alternate hook variations for each, focusing on a single, specific pain point or insight relevant to the client's audience. Read all scripts aloud to ensure natural delivery and confirm they are under 75 seconds.
* **Wednesday (1.5 hours): Batch Filming.** Film all 5-7 pieces in one session. Capture A-roll (talking to camera) and diverse B-roll footage (wide, close up, POV angles of 8-10 distinct actions) to provide dynamic visuals and multiple editing options. Change outfits 1-2 times to vary appearance across posts.
* **Thursday (0.5 hour): Organization & Prep.** Organize all raw footage using a strict folder hierarchy. Sync external audio, apply a consistent brand LUT, and export clips, preparing them for editing (whether self-edited or outsourced).
* **Output Cadence:** Post 4-5 Reels and 1-2 Carousels per week on Instagram, distributing these posts daily. Supplement with 1 long-form YouTube video every two weeks to build deeper trust and accumulate watch time.
* **Repurposing & Leverage:** Take one core idea and reshoot it across 2-3 angles (e.g., tutorial, myth-bust, transformation). Film the main body once, then record 5-10 alternate hooks to test. Reuse B-roll clips across different content by varying message, sound, or trend integration. Ensure every Carousel incorporates trending audio to maximize potential reach into the Reels tab.

## The 60-Second Pre-Post Checklist

1. Verify the verbal, written, and visual hooks are simultaneously present in the first three seconds.
2. Confirm audio levels peak below -6dB and sound clear, without clipping.
3. Check on-screen text for bold caps, word-by-word captions, and color-coded emphasis.
4. Ensure the content delivers one clear, focused idea or solution.
5. Confirm a single, clear verbal call to action is present and opens a curiosity loop.
6. Does the hook lead with a specific problem, contrarian take, or brand outlier for your niche?
7. Is there a visual cut or dynamic element every 2-3 seconds to maintain engagement?
8. Is background music strategically chosen, low in volume, and matched to emotional pacing?
9. Are relevant SEO keywords and 3-5 hashtags included in the caption to aid discovery?
10. Proofread all on-screen text and captions for accuracy and typos.
