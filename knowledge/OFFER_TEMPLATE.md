# The offer

Copy to `OFFER.md`. Read by the closer-review and sales-call-intelligence tools: they cannot
tell a good close from a bad one without knowing what was being sold and on what terms.

Plain sentences, no marketing. Cover:

- **What it is.** The deliverable and the length of the engagement.
- **The price**, and every tier that exists, including any cohort or promotional price that
  is live at the same time. If two prices are live at once, say so: a reviewer that does not
  know will report it as a defect on every call.
- **Payment plans.** Length, and what the monthly comes to.
- **Deposits.** The amounts that exist and when each is used.
- **Any revenue share**, with the threshold and the condition attached to it.
- **How it is sold.** Webinar into a one-to-one call, application, direct, and who runs it.

Update this the day the terms change. A review written against last month's pricing quietly
scores every closer against the wrong number.
