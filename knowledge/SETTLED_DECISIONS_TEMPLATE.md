# Settled decisions

Copy to `SETTLED_DECISIONS.md`. Read FIRST by the sales-call intelligence brief, every run.

## Why this file exists

The first run of that tool against a real client reported four deliberate decisions as
defects: two live prices, no price shown on the webinar, an automation claim, and a scarcity
line. All four were choices, all four had to be corrected by hand, and the corrections buried
the two findings that actually mattered.

A brief that re-opens a settled decision is worse than a brief that says nothing, because
someone has to read all of it to find out.

## What goes in it

One line per decision, with the reason. The reason is the point: a rule without one gets
argued with again in three weeks.

- **[The decision].** [Why it was made.] Not up for re-opening.

## What is worth reporting

List this too, so the brief has something to aim at rather than just things to avoid:

- Objections that appear on more than one call and are not handled anywhere in the funnel.
- Language buyers use that the marketing does not.
- Anything a closer promises that the fulfilment does not deliver.
- Where in the call the outcome is actually decided.

## The escape hatch

If something in this file has genuinely stopped being true, the brief should say so
explicitly and show the evidence for it. Settled is not permanent, it just is not free to
re-open.
