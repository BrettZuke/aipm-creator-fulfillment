10. **Law of Archetype.** One case study per ICP subcategory. Send-on-booking automation routes the matching one to the matching booking.

12. **Law of Tracking.** Per-video cash, not views. If you don't know which video brought £20k vs £200, you're flying blind.

## Preamble — The Whole Game

The job of marketing is to **shorten the sales cycle**. The job of the system is to deliver ~6 hours of content with us, ~11 touchpoints, across ~4 platforms — fast — install the right beliefs in the right order, and convert.

Three structural truths shape every decision:

1. **Trust is at an all-time low.** AI slop, recycled offers, scams, overhyped marketing. Pure-info offers are dying. The operators who win in 2026+ layer software, DFY, or an AI-agent component on top of info, and build a *knowable* personal brand (vulnerability + proof, not flex).
2. **Content has compounding leverage.** Paid ads underperform without the content ecosystem behind them. Cold DMs stay cold. Closers stay overworked. Once content is dialed in, "the closer becomes a cashier."
3. **The system has 8 layers, not 1.** Content + funnel + sales process + sales management + tracking + onboarding + service delivery + team. A single layer can't scale alone. Anyone selling you "just post more reels" is missing the other seven.

This document is the playbook for all 8 layers.

## Section 1 — The 12 Laws (full version)

### Law 1 — Gravity
**Rule:** When two unrelated operators who don't work together both teach the same thing, treat it as gravity.
**Why:** Independent convergence is the strongest signal that exists short of running the test ourselves. Saves months of A/B time.
**How to apply:** Adopt gravity rules without testing. Test only the things one source alone teaches. Catalog disagreements between sources for separate experimentation.

### Law 2 — Six Hours
**Rule:** Buyers need ~$1,000 of content per hour consumed. £4–6k offer = ~6 hours. £25k = 30+ short-form + 10–15 long-form in 72 hours. £500–£1,000 ticket = ~90 min webinar.
**Why:** Trust accrues with time-on-screen. Skim-and-buy is over.
**How to apply:** Engineer the funnel so a serious prospect can consume 6 hours in 72 hours. Means: foundational long-forms ready before a serious push. Means: engagement-campaign retargeting fires the moment they book.

### Law 3 — Four Formats
**Rule:** There are four content formats that sell coaching. Anyone who tells you there are more is wrong.
- **Raw Sit-Down** — sells the person (connection, loyalty, objection-killer)
- **Vlog** — sells the world (lifestyle visualization, identity-buying)
- **Value Sit-Down** — sells the brain (authority, mechanism belief)
- **Case Study** — sells the proof (removes "will this work for me")
**Why:** Each format kills one specific reason a prospect would say no. Rotating all four covers every doubt.
**How to apply:** Section 2 below. Every long-form is tagged with its format on the production calendar.

### Law 4 — Cycle, Not Calendar
**Rule:** Post the format that solves the current sales bottleneck, not what the calendar says.
**Why:** A pre-set Tuesday-is-value, Friday-is-vlog calendar wastes weeks when the funnel is bleeding somewhere specific. Content has a job.
**How to apply:** Friday huddle every week — read the bottleneck, pick next week's format. (Bottleneck → format mapping in Section 3.)

### Law 5 — Visual First
**Rule:** On short-form, the first frame decides whether they watch. Words don't have time.
**Why:** Dopamine-driven scrolls. By the time the audio registers, they've swiped.
**How to apply:**
- Phone, not fancy camera. A polished studio sit-down reads as "selling me" and gets swiped.
- Status-gap framing in the first frame: before/after, location contrast, time-of-day contrast, money-signal vs old-life-signal.
- The most viral example in our reference set: an operator filmed himself in first class while economy passengers walked past. The *frame* did the work; the words were almost incidental. Result: 2.7M views, 80k+ comments-as-leads, $100k+ in 1–3 months downstream.

### Law 6 — Microniche
**Rule:** Specificity beats reach. £-per-view, not views.
**Why:** A microniche video gets 500 views and converts 3 buyers. A broad video gets 50,000 views and converts 1. The algorithm rewards consistent narrow watch behavior, and the audience is so similar the recommendations compound fast.
**How to apply:**
- Treat YouTube as a search engine. Title = exact thing the ICP would type at midnight.
- Subniche specificity in titles: "How a UK plumber added £2k/mo in 8 weeks" beats "make money with AI."
- Bio promise is sharp enough that broke / wrong-fit people self-select out.

### Law 7 — Outcome, Not Mechanism
**Rule:** Sell the outcome. Gatekeep the mechanism. Information is the wrapping; the new opportunity is the gift.
**Why:** Buyers buy outcomes. Mechanisms are commodity (anyone can ChatGPT them). The mechanism is what they pay to learn — but only after the outcome has pulled them in.
**How to apply:**
- IG: tease the *what*, hide the *how*. "Show the lifestyle, gatekeep the how. People will DM to learn how."
- YouTube: teach the mechanism but always tied to the outcome (case-study sprinkles inside every value sit-down).
- Reframe content topics to mirror the offer's outcome — strip surface niche jargon. (Example from our reference set: a content coach was selling on hooks/views/algorithm — all mechanism. Re-pivoted to cash-collected/clients/business growth — pure outcome. Same offer 75x'd to $52k cash months in 60 days.)

### Law 8 — Seventy-Eighty
**Rule:** Long-form should do 70–80% of the selling. The sales call is a lay-up.
**Why:** If sales calls are doing all the work, content stack is broken. Dropping closers into a deficit makes them quit, kills close rate, and costs unit economics.
**How to apply:**
- A booked-but-unconvinced call = belief-shift content gap. Film raw sit-downs that pre-handle the objection.
- A call's first 10 minutes is for *confirming* the buyer's beliefs, not installing them. If the closer is teaching the offer mid-call, the content failed upstream.

### Law 9 — Telepathy
**Rule:** Strip surface-level niche jargon. Speak so directly the buyer feels you're pulling thoughts out of their head.
**Why:** Generic copy floats over the audience. Telepathic copy "calls them by name." Telepathic copy makes the prospect feel like the only logical option.
**How to apply:**
- Mine sales-call recordings, onboarding-form answers, and DMs for *exact* buyer phrasing. Use those phrases verbatim.
- Test: does the prospect feel like you've "pulled thoughts out of their head"? If yes, telepathic. If they're nodding politely, generic.

### Law 10 — Archetype
**Rule:** Make one case study per ICP subcategory. When someone books a call, send the case study that matches their archetype.
**Why:** A buyer projects themselves into a case study only if they see themselves in it. "If he can do it, I can do it" requires *similar* he. A general case study converts every archetype at half the rate of a matched case study.
**How to apply:**
- Identify ICP subcategories at offer-design time (typically 3–5 per offer).
- Film a 6-step case study (backstory → problem → what changed → process → result → why) per archetype.
- Booking automation routes matching case study to matching booking via tags / typeform answer.

### Law 11 — Stories
**Rule:** Daily IG stories with a 7-day rotation. Stories are the conversion engine; feed posts are awareness.
**Why:** Stories feel like 1:1 FaceTime. Parasocial bond accelerates. Story CTAs feel intimate, not pushy. Reel CTAs burn the algorithm.
**How to apply:** The 7-day rotation in Section 5.

### Law 12 — Tracking
**Rule:** Per-video cash, not views. Per-source, per-rep, per-video — every metric.
**Why:** If you don't know what brought the cash, you can't make more of what works. Operators flying without metrics fire on emotion and burn winners.
**How to apply:** Dashboard before scale (Section 8). Single non-negotiable: every booking has a source tag traceable to the YouTube video / IG post / ad / DM that generated it.

## Section 2 — The Four Formats (canonical detail)

### Format 1 — Raw Sit-Down (sells the person)

**Purpose:** Connection. Loyalty. Pre-handle the objection that's costing the sale.
**When to use:** Leads coming in but not closing. Emotional/identity gap is the problem.
**Style:** Phone propped up. Lowest edit. No script. No teacher mode. Like sitting across from the viewer at a pub.
**Length:** 15–45 minutes.

**Subtopics (every channel needs all of these over time):**
- **MY STORY video** (the foundational asset — non-negotiable). Origin, why we left X, the moment Y clicked, why most people will never get out and what's different now. Without this, vlogs and value videos lack an emotional anchor. Reference data: a creator with 300k IG followers and three YouTube videos sat at 40% show rate. The day he filmed his story, the funnel lit up — buyers DM'd "I fully connected to you when I watched that."
- **Limiting belief breaker.** Target a specific belief that costs sales. ("Why waiting until you're ready will keep you broke.")
- **Anti-flex reinvestment story.** "I reinvested $X into the business instead of [shiny purchase]" — pre-handles the "you should be flexing" objection by inverting it.
- **Objection-killer.** Pulled directly from a sales-call recording. The objection that just cost a deal becomes next week's video.

**Distribution:** Clip 5+ short-form pieces from one raw sit-down. All CTA back to the long-form via ManyChat keyword auto-DM ("DM me STORY for the link") or pinned comment.

### Format 2 — Vlog (sells the world)

**Purpose:** Lifestyle visualization. Identity-buying. Creates *positive pain* — the gap between viewer's current state and dream state. Pulls them down a rabbit hole of value/case-study content.
**When to use:** Calls happening but close rate low. Emotional/identity gap. Or during a launch.
**Critical rule:** NOT day-in-the-life with no purpose. NOT to bring leads in. To nurture leads already in the world.
**Style:** Phone, handheld, follow-along. Plant a curiosity question in the first 5 seconds that resolves over the video.

**Checklist (every vlog includes most of these):**
- Routine, environment, freedom-of-time signals
- Social proof in motion: a payment notification on screen, a student win, a Slack thread popping
- Personality: friends, banter, brotherhood
- A live moment: closing a student, debugging their build
- A raw-talk-to-camera moment in the middle (relatability sprinkle)
- A walked-back moment that ties to the viewer's current life ("I used to work at this fast food spot…")

**Hook formulas:**
- "[Aspirational thing] at [age]. How?"
- "[Anti-flex thing] because of [mechanism]"
- "Why my mate the [old-job] thinks I've gone mad"

**Don't give the sauce.** End with CTA to the value sit-down or free training.

### Format 3 — Value Sit-Down (sells the brain)

**Purpose:** Authority. Search-traffic capture. Belief installation in the mechanism.
**When to use:** Low leads, low DMs, awareness problem. Top of funnel.
**Style:** Filmed in a clean, repeatable spot. Light scripting via bullet points, NOT word-for-word. Heavy edit fine here (search intent — viewer commits to watch).
**Length:** 25–60 minutes.

**The 10-step structure (memorize):**
1. **Promise stack** (1 min): "If you watch the next 30 minutes, I'll show you exactly…"
2. **Proof stack** (2 min): dashboards, screenshots, results — sell them on watching first
3. **Before-state of client/you** (3 min): use a named pattern label for what was wrong ("the Miami guru effect," "the trades trap")
4. **Bridge:** install belief that the change wasn't luck — it was a system
5. **Mechanism reveal:** the named framework with N components
6. **Cycle / when-to-use chart:** which component to deploy when
7. **In-depth on each component** with examples and screenshots
8. **Recap:** show the actual posting timeline of the case-study client
9. **Big-picture ecosystem:** position this as one piece of a bigger system
10. **Clean CTA:** filter, don't sell. "Book a call. Not for shoppers. For people who want to grow."

**Sub-styles:**
- **Full-concept breakdown** (whole framework, free-course energy)
- **Nuggetfy** (one problem, one short answer — give it all away over time, no one wants to wait, so they buy)

**Hard rule:** Never just teach. Prove every concept through a case-study sprinkle inside the video. Tie every concept back to the offer's outcome.

### Format 4 — Case Study (sells the proof)

**Purpose:** Remove the "will this work for me?" doubt. Buyer projects themselves in.
**When to use:** Good views but no calls booked. Or before/during a launch.
**Critical timing rule:** Case studies BEFORE and DURING a launch, not after. Build audience 3 weeks pre-launch.

**The two questions every buyer has:**
1. Do I trust this person? (Raw sit-down kills this.)
2. Do I believe this will work for me? (Case study kills this.)

**The 6-step arc (memorize):**
1. Backstory
2. Problem
3. What changed
4. The process
5. The result
6. Why it worked

**Production sub-styles:**
- **Interview style** (king): client talks 80%, host facilitates 20%. Trust is built through their voice, not yours. "Stop talking, let them talk."
- **Build-out style:** sit next to the client, break down their business live. Captures their reactions to belief-shifts in real time.

**The hidden-ICP play:** Make a separate case study for each ICP subcategory. Send the matching one when someone books a call (Law 10).

**Cash-collected proof:** Case studies run forever. Reference data point — one case-study video generated $18k in one filter window, $28k in revenue, $83k cash collected over its lifetime. Track per-video cash (Law 12).

## Section 3 — The Cycle (bottleneck → format)

**Friday huddle. Read the bottleneck. Pick the format that fixes it. Film next week.**

| Symptom | Diagnosis | Format to deploy |
|---|---|---|
| Low leads, low DMs, low traffic | Awareness problem | **Value Sit-Down** + clip into short-form. Microniche. Free-course energy. Reframe the ICP's exact problem. |
| Decent views, no calls booked | Interest without conviction | **Raw Sit-Down** (objection-killer pulled from sales calls) + **Case Study** (subcategory match). |
| Calls booked but flaky/unconvinced | Belief gap before call. Long-form not selling enough. | **Raw Sit-Down** (belief-shifter) + deeper mechanism breakdown. Aim for 70/80 (Law 8). |
| Calls happening, close rate low | Emotional/identity gap. They get it, don't feel it. | **Vlog** (lifestyle visualization) + **Case Study** (transformation arc). |

This matrix is the operating system. When a sales week is bad, the answer is in this table.

## Section 4 — The Hook System

### Visual hooks (most important — first frame decides on short-form)

Status gaps that work universally:
- **Job vs freedom:** old uniform/vehicle in foreground → new environment in foreground
- **Time of day:** filming at 10:30am Tuesday with explicit callout ("if you were on a normal job right now…")
- **Place:** filming from somewhere most of the audience can't be at that hour (a pub mid-afternoon, a school playground at pick-up, a beach in October, a café in a different country)
- **Money signals (subtle):** Stripe notification on screen, payslip vs Stripe payout side-by-side
- **Anti-flex flex:** Same old vehicle/clothes, different life — explicitly called out

Filming rule: **phone over fancy camera on short-form.** Polished sit-downs read as guru-bait. Raw and on-the-move outperforms cinematic in 2025+.

### Verbal hook formulas (open with one of these)

- **"How [client] went from [low] to [high] (without [common shortcut])"**
- **"I [outcome] in [timeframe] with [unusual constraint]"**
- **"Why I quit [normal path] and made [outcome]"**
- **"Don't [common action] until you [milestone]"**
- **"This should be required viewing if you want to [outcome]"**
- **"I reinvested [amount] into [thing] instead of [shiny object]"**
- **"Why [normal belief] is killing your [desired outcome]"**
- **"How [unexpected thing] got me [desired outcome]"**

### Title rules

- Specific number > general claim. £2,400 > "a lot." 28 days > "fast."
- Brackets for personality / disqualifiers: *(Without Tech Skills)* / *(Free Course)* / *(UK-Only)*
- Lowercase = relatability for some audiences (younger / online-coded). Test before defaulting — older / trades audiences may read it as kid energy.
- Microniche specificity (Law 6) — the exact ICP search query.

### Thumbnail rules

- Always include the founder's face (no shy account)
- Include a result or contrast (van → laptop, payslip → Stripe)
- Quality is non-negotiable. Hire someone good. Reference rule: "If they see your video quality sucks, they perceive you as lower status. They think this is how you'd treat their service delivery."

### First 30 seconds (long-form opening)

Open with the result. Then the credibility stack (proof, dashboards). Then transition to the before-state of the journey. Don't bury the lede.

Template: *"If you give me the next [N] minutes, I'll show you exactly how [ICP outcome]. I'm going to break down [list of 6 things]. So sit back and let's get into it."*

## Section 5 — The 7-Day Story Rotation

Stories are the conversion engine. Run this daily. Predictability kills story views — mix the order monthly, but the categories stay constant.

| Day | Theme | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Client win + hard CTA | "[Student] just hit [number]. DM me '[KEYWORD]' if you want the same setup." |
| Tue | Value + reveal a hidden problem | "Most people fail because [hidden cause]. Here's what to do instead…" |
| Wed | Personal story (vulnerable) | Origin moment, future goal, big risk taken, doubt admitted |
| Thu | Own win | Payment notification screenshot, student message dump, dashboard reveal |
| Fri | Soft CTA / free resource | "DM me '[KEYWORD]' for the [free thing] I gave my paying students" |
| Sat | Free format / behind-the-scenes | A walk, a coffee, what we're working on, real life |
| Sun | Free format / Q&A | "Reply with your biggest [topic] question and I'll answer" |

**Rules:**
- Always give more than you take
- Soft CTAs every day, hard CTAs only Mon
- ManyChat keyword auto-DM on Mon and Fri keywords
- Close-friends list for OG followers gets a separate booking link during launches (bumps them to top of priority queue)

## Section 6 — The Funnel Stack

Eight specific upgrades. Each is independently leveraged — install all eight.

### 1. Pre-call page (3 framing videos)

After booking, prospect lands on a thank-you page with three videos under 3 minutes each:
- **Questions video** — what to expect, what to bring, what to have ready
- **Objections video** — every recurring close-killer pre-handled with belief shift
- **Case studies + expectations** — 30/60/90 day journey walkthrough integrated with current testimonials

Plus: top-of-page instructions to click calendar invite YES (+5% show rate from this micro-commitment alone). Bottom-of-page: stack of real talking-head testimonials matching ICP archetypes — NOT Canva fakes.

### 2. Embed Calendly inside Typeform

Single biggest funnel fix. Sending qualified applications to a separate Calendly drops ~50% of bookings. Embed the calendar inside the typeform after qualification → 100% qualified-to-booked rate.

### 3. The 18-emails-in-72-hours sequence ("Hammer Them")

After booking, fire **6 emails per day for 3 days**. Cycle through 4 content sections:
1. **Questions answered** — what they need to know to show up
2. **Second-layer questions** — the follow-ups the first answer triggers ("if I told you 3 hours/day, you'd ask 3 hours on what?")
3. **Objections + FAQs** — pre-handle recurring close-killers
4. **Expectations + case studies** — process walkthrough integrated with student wins

Plus 4 SMS touches: 24h out, 6h out, 1h out, when call is live.

Result: shorter sales calls because everything's pre-handled. Higher show rate. Closer arrives as cashier, not teacher.

### 4. The Group Chat Method

The moment setter qualifies + confirms:
1. Setter creates iMessage group with setter + closer + lead
2. **NEVER green text** — must be blue iMessage. Use Sendblue or equivalent if sending from non-iOS systems
3. Setter introduces themselves, can use AI evergreen voice clone of founder with name plug-in (11 Labs)
4. Closer drops a **personalized talking-head video** (not text) — using setter's notes from Slack
5. Bait a response: "I have a video that solves [their specific problem from notes]. Send me a quick text confirming this is your number and I'll send it."

The personalised video using actual notes is the magic — it's the closer's "first impression" before the call even starts.

### 5. Pre-call popup confirmation

On Calendly: "We only work with serious [ICP] who respect their time. Click YES to confirm you'll show up." Reference data: +5% show rate inside 3 days, multiple extra closes/week on a $25k offer.

### 6. Engagement campaign ($500–$1,000 / 72 hours)

Minute someone books → drop them into a Meta retargeting audience for 72 hours. They see ~30–45 short-form pieces and 10–15 long-form pieces in feed before the call. Bucket the ads: problem, solution, opportunity, lifestyle, case-study. Pennies-on-the-dollar lift. Run continuously in the background.

### 7. Per-video cash tracking

Build the dashboard (Replit, Airtable, or HighLevel custom):
- UTM per YouTube video
- Source-tracking on every booking, every closed deal
- Display: views | opt-ins | qualified | booked | shows | closed cash — per video

Find the videos doing $20k vs $200. Make more of the first ones. Kill or rework the rest.

### 8. Source-switching tactic

If the dashboard shows IG-sourced bookings show up at lower rates than YouTube-sourced bookings, force IG leads through a YouTube long-form before they can book. Add "watch this 30-min training first" in the DM flow. Pulls show rate up to YouTube parity.

## Section 7 — The Production System

### Team hire order (build in this sequence)

| Stage | Hire | Function | Cost guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | **Editor** | Long-form cuts + short-form clips. No animations. Captions clean (CapCut classic / Tiny Lemon Milk). | £600–£1,500/mo overseas |
| 30 days | **Scriptwriter / VA** | Draft bullet outlines from sales-call recordings + onboarding forms. Draft story sequences. Handle posting. | £800–£2,500/mo |
| 30 days | **Thumbnail designer** | Just thumbnails. Quality is non-negotiable. | £30–£60/thumb freelance |
| Pre-launch | **DM setter** | Manage all DMs. Qualify, book, warm outbound. | £1,500–£3,000/mo or commission |
| ~£20k/mo | **Closer** (separate from founder) | Take the calls. Frees the founder to be the brand, not the salesman. | 10–15% commission |
| ~£20k/mo | **Project manager** (Philippines) | Make sure team delivers on each new client / launch | £1,000–£1,500/mo |
| ~£25k/mo | **CSM** | Client communication via Slack/Discord, status tracking | £1,200–£1,500/mo |

The founder's job at scale: be the brand + decide direction. Not film, edit, post, or close.

### Cadence

- **YouTube long-form:** 1–2 per week minimum. "If you are not posting on YouTube at least one time every 5 days, you are not going to succeed."
- **Short-form:** post daily. **Never batch.** "Make a video, post it the same day, get the views, adjust, make the next one better." Tight feedback loop > batching.
- **IG stories:** daily, 7-day rotation
- **Newsletter:** weekly, repurposed from existing YT/IG content

### Filming setup

- **Long-form:** batch 2–4 videos every fortnight in a clean repeatable spot. Same outfit, same setup. Saves the founder's calendar.
- **Short-form:** phone, on-the-move, real life. NOT in the studio.
- **Studio:** for podcasts and YouTube only. Not daily short-form (setup overhead kills cadence).

### The repurposing pipeline (every long-form)

1. Founder films 30–60 min long-form
2. Editor cuts long-form for YouTube + extracts 6–10 short-form clips
3. Each clip ends with "full video on YouTube — search '[exact title]'" or ManyChat keyword
4. VA writes newsletter summary from the long-form
5. VA creates 5–7 IG story slides previewing the newsletter / driving people to YouTube
6. Founder's filming output = 8 days of distribution

### Tool stack

- **HighLevel** — CRM, automations, calendar, SMS
- **Calendly / Tally** — bookings (Calendly embedded inside Typeform)
- **Sendblue** — blue iMessage from CRM
- **Airtable / Replit** — sales tracking dashboard with per-video cash
- **Fathom / Otter** — sales call recording (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

## Section 8 — The Tracking Doctrine

### Mandatory metrics + benchmarks

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Opt-in rate | 30%+ |
| Application completion | 60%+ |
| Qualified app → booked call | 100% (achievable via embed) |
| Show rate (organic) | 70–80% |
| Show rate (paid) | 50%+ |
| Close rate (organic) | 50%+ |
| Close rate (paid) | 30%+ |
| Pickup rate | doubled with 5-min rule + area-code spoofer |
| Disqualification rate | <5% |
| Payment processor fees | <5% (audit if higher; switch processor) |

### Per-video cash tracking (kingmaker)

The dashboard non-negotiable. Per YouTube video and per IG post:
- Views
- Opt-ins
- Qualified leads
- Booked calls
- Show / no-show
- Closed cash
- Cash per view

Make more of the videos doing £20k+. Kill or rework the £200 videos.

### The "double 5 rates = 32x revenue" math

If 100 leads/wk, $1k offer, with 5 OK rates yielding $540 total → doubling each of 5 rates → $17,280/wk. **32x leverage** from 5 rate improvements. Fix the rates before fixing the lead volume.

### Sales call recordings (idea mine — non-negotiable)

Every sales call recorded (Fathom / Otter). Required questions on every call:
- "Why did you book a call with US specifically vs anyone else?"
- "What sets our brand apart?"
- "What was the final thing that pushed you over the edge to book?"
- "Who else are you watching right now? What do you like / dislike about their marketing?"

Buyer's exact wording becomes hook + script language. Patterns become content topics.

### Onboarding form (post-purchase — idea mine)

Every new buyer fills out:
1. Why did you buy from us specifically vs any other offer?
2. What sets our brand apart?
3. What was the final thing that pushed you over the edge?
4. Who else are you watching? What do you like / dislike about their marketing?
5. What are the 3 problems you want solved in the next 90 days?
6. What are the biggest fears and doubts behind your goal?
7. What's the ONE thing that if achieved right now would make you feel satisfied?
8. What does your current situation and day-to-day look like?
9. What would have pushed you over the edge to take action sooner?

Better: have them record themselves answering on video. More context, exact wording, natural language.

## Section 9 — The 24-Day Launch System

Replicable revenue engine. Run 4–8 times per year depending on audience fatigue tolerance.

### Days 1–3 — Mysterious Hype (3 IG stories/day + email)

- Day 1: "Something big is coming back tomorrow"
- Day 2: Show authority numbers without revealing offer ($50k in 30 days, etc)
- Day 3: Build scarcity ("20 spots will be available, waitlist members get 24h early access")

### Days 4–10 — Waitlist (Proof of Concept Selling)

Sell during the waitlist, not just after. Why "Proof of Concept": (a) money fast, (b) test if public launch is worth running, (c) gather objection data, (d) spread out lead flow for sales team.

Story sequence:
- Personal story slide (humble starting numbers — makes audience correlate)
- Build scarcity (20 spots, waitlist opens 24h early)
- Question stories (Q&A box: "Got questions about the mentorship?") — handles objections publicly
- Close-friends story (premium track for OGs gets separate booking link)
- Spam student results
- DM "[keyword]" to apply → dialers call same day, book qualified leads same day

### Days 11–17 — Public Launch (Cohort)

Use waitlist data to revise sales process, marketing, backend. Story sequence shifts to:
- "Public launch begins — 20 spots"
- Voice spam results (live + past)
- "[N] applications" reveal (people don't feel weird applying when others are too — flip camera, show screen)
- Live call screen shares (FOMO from inner circle calls)
- Belief shifting + perspective shifts
- "Last chance — [X] of 20 spots left"

1–2 YouTube videos posted with mentorship CTAs. Email sequence runs in parallel.

### Days 18–24 — Free Training Funnel

Audience is sold-out / fatigued. Switch to free training (VSSL disguised):
- Contact wall to access training (name, email, phone)
- Below the video: same offer application
- Story sequence: "DMs of people saying training is valuable"
- Q&A stories where answers are "in the free training"
- Sales team calls every opt-in

The free-training funnel ends the launch on continued lead capture and bridges to evergreen.

## Section 10 — ICP Doctrine

### Dream Follower vs ICP

- **ICP** = the buyer (e.g., "people stuck at $3–$20k/mo wanting $30–50k/mo")
- **Dream follower** = the broader TAM that contains the ICP (e.g., "people in the online business space")

Top-of-funnel content targets dream followers. Inside the dream-follower pool sits a higher-density ICP, so when content rips, you both grow audience AND get more buyers — without sacrificing the right type of follower.

### The ICP discovery workflow

Every offer fills out a "Dream follower + ICP sheet" before content planning. Iterate the sheet using:
- Patterns from sales calls (Section 8)
- Onboarding form answers from new students (Section 8)
- DMs (where people are more raw, more emotional, more honest than on calls)
- Market research calls with paying customers

### Microniche specificity (Law 6 applied)

- Headline calls out specific revenue range / role / region
- Calendar carries a friction question: "If you have under [£X], you are not financially qualified to work with us. Do not book."
- This DOES turn away leads. Some people text saying "I didn't book because I saw who you were targeting." That's the point.

### Archetype case studies (Law 10 applied)

Identify ICP subcategories at offer-design time. For most coaching offers: 3–5 archetypes. Film one case study per archetype using the 6-step arc (Section 2, Format 4). Set up booking automation to send the matching one to the matching booking via tags / typeform answer.

## Section 11 — Mistakes (Don't Do These)

### Content mistakes
- Calendar-driven posting instead of bottleneck-driven (Law 4)
- Day-in-the-life vlogs with no purpose
- Hyperedited "Iman Gadzhi" studio reels on short-form — reads as guru-bait
- Studio for daily short-form (overhead kills cadence)
- Filming on a fancy camera for short-form — too polished = "trying to sell me"
- Talking to "all business owners" / "all coaches" — too broad
- Generic "best practices" content with no archetype
- Selling the mechanism instead of the outcome (Law 7)
- Free-value-only with no offer mention ever
- Same format every video — algorithm starves
- 720p Loom videos with browser tabs visible (kills perceived status)
- Posting any content with no motive

### Funnel mistakes
- Putting a book-a-call funnel in IG link-in-bio (cold). Use a YouTube long-form / VSL instead.
- No pre-call page — just bare Calendly
- Sending qualified app to a separate page (drops 50% of bookings — embed Calendly)
- Letting unqualified leads onto the calendar (cancel rate too high)
- Missing partial-submit on typeform (lose leads on the budget question)
- No headline / VSSL targeting specific revenue range — broke leads pollute funnel
- Application instead of direct calendar when lead flow is too low (over-friction)
- Typeform alone with no Slack / automation = setters call too late, lose 5-min rule

### Sales mistakes
- Pitching for 30 min instead of B2C-style discovery
- Mentioning results in DMs (let highlights say it)
- Green text messages from CRM (instant trust wall)
- No group chat method (lower show rate)
- Closer doing generic intro instead of personalized talking-head
- Not tracking per-rep metrics (firing on emotion)
- No competitive environment for sales team
- Not paying setter / closer commissions properly

### Tracking mistakes
- Tracking nothing
- Tracking views (not cash) per video
- Total close rate only (need per-source, per-rep, per-video)
- No EOD form for dialers
- Not tracking objections (can't iterate VSSL)
- Not tracking show rate per platform

### Onboarding mistakes
- No automation between close and Discord / Slack
- No contract / Trello pipeline
- No onboarding form to harvest content insights
- No 1:1 channel ("group chats only" loses high-priority students)

### Service delivery mistakes
- Selling pure info without DFY templates / SOPs (trust recession kills this)
- Q&A-only group calls (people don't know what they don't know)
- No coaches per business sub-area (founder bottleneck)
- No leaderboard / competitive layer
- Building course before getting one-on-one calls' data → wrong content

### Mindset mistakes
- 2X mindset ("work harder") instead of 10X ("who can do this for me")
- Trying to scale as a "shadow operator" with no personal brand
- Glorified-freelancer thinking ("I'll build everything")
- Thinking lead-booked-a-call = ready to buy (they're only curious)
- Treating sales team as employees, not family (they get poached for $5k more elsewhere)
- Quitting after 3–6 months with no sales — "Whoever can stay in the market the longest, keep showing their authentic self, keep delivering results — you'll crush it."
- Listening to mentors to a tee — different markets, different audiences. Adapt, don't copy.

## Section 13 — Intake Protocol (Adding New Sources)

When new growth-op research enters our system (a podcast, video, book, interview, course teardown):

1. **Pull the transcript** (`tools/youtube/youtube_scraper.py` for YouTube; equivalent for other platforms)
2. **Run the extraction agent** producing a playbook in the standard 12-section template (see `.tmp/growth_op_analysis/jakub_playbook.md` and `jonas_playbook.md` for the template)
3. **Compare against current doctrine.** Three outcomes:
   - **Confirms an existing rule** → add to Field Log as supporting evidence; increment confidence; no doctrine change needed
   - **Adds a new rule not yet in doctrine** → propose it before adding (don't bloat doctrine on one source). New rules require either independent corroboration (Law 1) OR a successful field test (Section 12) before promotion to doctrine
   - **Contradicts an existing rule** → flag for review. Run a test (Section 12). Don't change doctrine on one contrary source
4. **Update Sources appendix** with provenance (creator, video URL, ingest date)
5. **Move raw playbook to `.tmp/growth_op_analysis/<creator>_playbook.md`** for re-reference
