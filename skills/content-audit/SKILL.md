# /content-audit — Content Improvement System

10 interactive prompts for improving your content. Each one asks you questions first, then delivers something actionable.

## How to use

Tell me which number you want, or describe what you need and I'll pick the right one.

---

## The 10 Prompts

**1. Post Performance Audit**
Analyze my last 10 posts and tell me exactly what's working, what's not, and what type of content I should be making more of based on my engagement data.
→ *Paste your post titles/captions and their engagement numbers. I'll tell you the pattern.*

**2. Competitor Gap Analysis**
Look at the top 3 creators in my niche right now. Tell me what content formats they're using that I'm not — and give me 5 specific ideas to test this week.
→ *Tell me your niche and who the top 3 are. I'll do the analysis.*

**3. Hook Rewriter**
Take this video script and rewrite the hook 5 different ways. Make each one feel completely different in tone and angle. Then tell me which one you'd put money on and why.
→ *Paste your hook or opening line. I'll give you 5 rewrites + a verdict.*

**4. Viral Angle Finder**
Give me 3 content angles for my business that feel counterintuitive or surprising — the kind of thing that makes someone screenshot it and send it to a friend.
→ *Tell me what your business does. I'll find the angles others aren't taking.*

**5. Calendar Audit**
Review my content calendar for this month. Tell me if I'm talking about myself too much, if I'm being too promotional, and if there are any big gaps in the kind of value I'm giving.
→ *Paste your content plan or list of topics. I'll audit the mix.*

**6. Hook Generator**
Write me 10 hooks for short-form video — for my niche — that tap into a real frustration my audience has. Make them feel visceral, not logical.
→ *Tell me your niche and your audience's biggest frustration. I'll write 10 hooks.*

**7. Content Repurposer**
Turn this YouTube video transcript into a 5-part Instagram carousel, a TikTok caption, and a short email. Make each one feel native to that platform.
→ *Paste your transcript or video summary. I'll give you all three formats.*

**8. Format Gap Finder**
What's the one content format I'm NOT using that has the highest potential for my audience right now? Explain why, give me an example, and write the first one for me.
→ *Tell me your niche and what formats you currently use. I'll find the gap.*

**9. 30-Day Content Plan**
Help me build a 30-day content plan. I want it to be 70% educational, 20% behind-the-scenes, and 10% promotional. Give me specific topics, not just categories.
→ *Tell me your niche, audience, and offer. I'll build the full plan.*

**10. Underperformer Fix**
Take this piece of content that underperformed and tell me exactly why it didn't land — and rewrite it so it has a real shot.
→ *Paste the content + its engagement numbers. I'll diagnose and rewrite it.*

---

## Instructions

1. Ask which prompt the user wants (or infer from context)
2. Ask only the questions specific to that prompt
3. Deliver something they can use immediately — copy, a plan, or a rewrite
4. No generic advice. Every output should be specific to their niche and audience.
