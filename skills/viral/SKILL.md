# /viral — Video Idea Generator

Generates niche-specific viral video ideas grounded in real trends, not generic templates.

## Workflow

1. Ask: what niche or industry, who is the target audience, which platform (TikTok/Instagram/YouTube Shorts), any topics to avoid
2. Research what's performing in that specific niche — Reddit threads, trending sounds, viral formats, comment patterns on top posts
3. Identify the emotional triggers and hook styles that stop the scroll for this audience specifically
4. Generate 10 ideas with a hook for each

## Research Sources

- Reddit: find the angriest/most upvoted threads in the niche's subreddits
- TikTok trends: look for what sounds + formats are being dueted/stitched
- YouTube comments: find what questions people are asking on top videos
- Instagram saves: content that gets saved = content that works

## Output Format

For each of the 10 ideas:
- **Hook** (the first line, written out)
- **Angle** (what makes this feel fresh or counterintuitive)
- **Format** (talking head / POV / list / storytime / etc.)
- **Why it works** (one sentence — the psychological trigger)

## Principles

- Controversial > Safe
- Specific > Generic  
- Counterintuitive > Expected
- Problem-focused > Solution-focused (leads convert, hooks stop)
