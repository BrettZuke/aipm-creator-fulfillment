# /content-director — Content Planner

Builds a complete, executable content plan based on your goals and audience.

## Workflow

1. Ask: business/offer, target audience, primary platform(s), content goal (followers / leads / sales / authority), posting frequency target, content you've already made that performed well
2. Audit what's working vs. what to stop
3. Build the calendar: topics, formats, mix ratios, posting days/times
4. Deliver a ready-to-execute plan

## Content Mix Framework

Default mix (adjust based on goals):
- **70% Educational** — teach something valuable, no pitch
- **20% Behind-the-scenes / Personal** — build trust, show the human
- **10% Promotional** — offer, CTA, direct ask

## Algorithm Principles

- **Consistency > volume** — 3x/week forever beats 7x/week for a month
- **Save rate > like rate** — saves signal evergreen value
- **Series > standalone** — multi-part content drives follows
- **Reply bait** — end with a question that's easy to answer

## Output

- Full calendar for the requested period (topic, format, platform, day)
- Rationale for the mix
- 3 "tent pole" pieces — the high-effort content that anchors the schedule
- Quick-win ideas that can be made in under 30 minutes
