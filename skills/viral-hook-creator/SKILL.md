# Viral Hook Creator

A hook is the first sentence someone reads or hears before they decide to keep going. Pick the pattern that fits the content, write the hook, then run it through the checklist.

## The 18 hook patterns

1. **Authority** — "As someone who's [credential], here's what most people get wrong about X."
2. **Contrarian** — "Everyone says X. They're wrong."
3. **Data-driven** — "I looked at 200 X and found this."
4. **Curiosity-gap** — "The one thing nobody tells you about X."
5. **Pattern-interrupt** — Open mid-action or mid-sentence, skip the wind-up entirely.
6. **Direct callout** — "If you're a [specific audience], read this."
7. **Confession** — "I used to believe X. I was wrong."
8. **Before/after** — "X used to take me 3 hours. Now it takes 10 minutes."
9. **Listicle promise** — "5 ways to do X that nobody talks about."
10. **Problem-agitate** — "X is costing you money and you don't know it yet."
11. **Story cold-open** — Start mid-scene: "I almost lost the client over this."
12. **Comparison** — "X vs Y: only one actually works."
13. **Specific question** — A precise, answerable question — never a generic "Have you ever...".
14. **Bold claim + proof tease** — "X is dead. Here's the data."
15. **Insider secret** — "What [industry] doesn't want you to know about X."
16. **Mistake callout** — "The biggest mistake I see people make with X."
17. **Timestamp specificity** — "In the last 30 days, X happened."
18. **Reverse-expectation** — Open with the outcome, then explain how you got there.

## Forces active voice

Every hook must have a named subject doing an action. No exceptions.

- Banned: "There is a problem with X." → Fixed: "X is broken."
- Banned: "X was discovered by researchers." → Fixed: "Researchers found X."
- Banned: "It was found that..." → Fixed: name who found it and what.

## Quality checklist

- One sentence, ideally under 12 words
- Names a specific audience, number, or detail — not vague language
- Creates a question the reader wants answered
- No throat-clearing ("In this post I will...")
- Passes the scroll-stop test: would a stranger stop on this line specifically, not just the topic
- No banned AI words or hedging (see `avoid-ai-writing` skill)
