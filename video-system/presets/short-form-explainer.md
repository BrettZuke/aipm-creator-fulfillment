# Preset: short-form explainer

9:16 vertical (1080x1920, 30fps). For explainer-style shorts (IG Reels, TikTok, YT Shorts).

Layout:
- Top half: the graphics zone. Every content beat gets a graphic here (kinetic title, diagram, data callout, screenshot pop).
- Bottom half: the speaker's face, cropped from the source 16:9 footage (track the face; keep it centered).
- Middle band (the seam between halves): captions.

Captions:
- Coolvetica font, white words, solid black text box behind them.
- Words animate in as spoken (word-level timestamps from the transcript).
- 2 to 4 words per line, never covering the mouth or the active graphic.

Graphics style:
- Bold, high-contrast, one focal element per beat.
- Enter and exit animations on everything; nothing pops.
- Brand PNGs and logos from assets/graphics/ when relevant.
