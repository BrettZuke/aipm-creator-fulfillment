# Preset: long-form YouTube

16:9 (1920x1080, 30fps source-matched). For full YouTube videos and intros.

Layout:
- Talking head stays full frame.
- Graphics are overlay cards composited ON the footage: lower thirds, side panels, data callouts, kinetic titles, PiP demo windows, animated diagrams. Position so the speaker's face stays clear.
- No burned-in captions by default (YouTube handles subtitles); on-screen text is part of the graphics, not a caption track.

Graphics style:
- One graphic per content beat; a beat is roughly one claim, step, or example in the transcript.
- Premium and clean: geometric light effects, glow, springs with settle. No clip-art, no emoji glyphs, SVG icons only.
- Payoff moments (a key number, a reveal) get a brief celebration animation so they land.
- Brand colors and mascot PNGs from assets/graphics/.

Music:
- Subtle bed, -18 to -23 dB under voice, fade out at the end.
