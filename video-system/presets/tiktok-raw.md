# Preset: TikTok raw

9:16 vertical (1080x1920, 30fps). Raw, native-feeling cut. The vibe is unpolished on purpose.

Layout:
- Text hook at the top for the first 2 to 4 seconds: one punchy line, plain white text with a slight shadow or black box, static or minimal pop-in.
- Full-frame raw footage underneath. No graphics zone.
- Captions in the lower third, same Coolvetica white-on-black-box style, word-by-word animate-in.

Editing style:
- Aggressive silence trimming, fast pacing, jump cuts are fine and expected.
- No motion graphics beyond the hook and captions. If a visual is needed, a quick full-screen PNG or screenshot flash, half a second, in and out.
